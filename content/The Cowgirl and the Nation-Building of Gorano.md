Characterizing Gorano:
- Vague aesthetic ideas:
	- Flowing lava rivers
		- Boats or ships that go along them, helping facilitate trade
		- Lava provides natural defense mechanism from bandits, 
			- However, there are magma mephits and other fire elementals -> get a bunch of script kiddies with Ray of Frost locked and loaded
		- The geysers result in a source of freshwater
			- Also, hot springs!
		- In a scientific sense we have geothermal energy, but in a setting with this level of technology, it makes sense that buildings and households are heated this way
			- Actually they can use steam power from this too—no coal burning needed
		- I would imagine that the volcanic (sulfuric) fumes are not good for residents—we'll probably have some sort of herbal concoction that gives immunity to these effects
			- Where would these plants be found, how would they be mass-produced?
