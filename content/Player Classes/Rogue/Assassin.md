> *<span style="color:rgb(125, 125, 125)">Practice the grim art of death.</span>*

An Assassin’s training focuses on the grim art of death, using stealth, poison, and disguise to eliminate foes with deadly efficiency. While some [[Rogue|Rogues]] who follow this path are hired killers, spies, or bounty hunters, the capabilities of this subclass are equally useful for adventurers facing a variety of monstrous enemies.

### Mechanical Traits

#### <span style="color:rgb(203, 123, 55)">Level 3: Assassinate</span> 

You are adept at getting the drop on a target, granting you the following benefits:

> **<span style="color:rgb(134, 93, 187)">Initiative</span>**: You have Advantage on Initiative rolls.
> 
> **<span style="color:rgb(134, 93, 187)">Surprising Strikes</span>**: During the first round of each combat, you have Advantage on attack rolls against any creature that hasn’t taken a turn. If your Sneak Attack hits any target during that round, the target takes extra damage of the weapon’s type equal to your Rogue level.

#### <span style="color:rgb(203, 123, 55)">Level 3: Assassin's Tools</span>

You gain proficiency with the [[Disguise Kit]] and [[Poisoner’s Kit]], along with the two physical kits.

#### <span style="color:rgb(203, 123, 55)">Level 9: Infiltration Expertise</span>

You are expert at posing as someone else to aid your infiltrations. While in a disguise created using your [[Disguise Kit]], you have Advantage on any Charisma (Deception) check you make while pretending to be someone else. You can also unerringly mimic another person’s speech, handwriting, or both if you have spent at least one hour studying each one.

#### <span style="color:rgb(203, 123, 55)">Level 13: Envenom Weapons</span>

When you use the <span style="color:rgb(134, 93, 187)">Poison</span> option of your [[Cunning Strike]], the target also takes 2d6 Poison damage whenever it fails the saving throw. This damage ignores resistance to Poison damage.

#### <span style="color:rgb(203, 123, 55)">Level 17: Death Strike</span>

When you hit with your [[Sneak Attack]] on the first round of a combat, the target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw (DC 8 + your Dexterity modifier + your Proficiency Bonus), or the attack’s damage is doubled against the target.
