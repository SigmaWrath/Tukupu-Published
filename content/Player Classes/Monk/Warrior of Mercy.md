> *<span style="color:rgb(125, 125, 125)">Manipulate forces of life and death</span>*

Warriors of Mercy manipulate the life force of others. These [[Monk|Monks]] are wandering physicians, but they bring a swift end to their enemies. Some wear masks, presenting themselves as faceless bringers of life and death.

### Mechanical Traits
#### <span style="color:rgb(203, 123, 55)">Level 3: Hand of Harm</span>
Once per turn when you hit a creature with an Unarmed Strike and deal damage, you can expend 1 Focus Point to deal extra Necrotic damage equal to one roll of your [[Martial Arts|Martial Arts die]] plus your Wisdom modifier.

#### <span style="color:rgb(203, 123, 55)">Level 3: Hand of Healing</span>
As a Magic action, you can expend 1 Focus Point to touch a creature and restore a number of Hit Points equal to a roll of your Martial Arts die plus your Wisdom modifier.

When you use your Flurry of Blows, you can replace one of the Unarmed Strikes with a use of this feature without expending a [[Monk’s Focus|Focus Point]] for the healing.

#### <span style="color:rgb(203, 123, 55)">Level 3: Implements of Mercy</span>  
You gain proficiency in the Insight and Medicine skills and proficiency with the [[Herbalism Kit]].

#### <span style="color:rgb(203, 123, 55)">Level 6: Physician's Touch</span>
Your <span style="color:rgb(203, 123, 55)">Hand of Harm</span> and <span style="color:rgb(203, 123, 55)">Hand of Healing</span> improve, as detailed below.

> ***<span style="color:rgb(134, 93, 187)">Hand of Harm</span>***: When you use <span style="color:rgb(203, 123, 55)">Hand of Harm</span> on a creature, you can also give that creature the Poisoned condition until the end of your next turn.
> 
> ***<span style="color:rgb(134, 93, 187)">Hand of Healing</span>***: When you use <span style="color:rgb(203, 123, 55)">Hand of Healing</span>, you can also end one of the following conditions on the creature you heal: [[Blinded]], [[Deafened]], [[Paralyzed]], [[Poisoned]], or [[Stunned]].

These benefits apply whenever you use those features.
#### <span style="color:rgb(203, 123, 55)">Level 11: Flurry of Healing and Harm </span>

When you use Flurry of Blows, you can replace each of the Unarmed Strikes with a use of <span style="color:rgb(203, 123, 55)">Hand of Healing</span> without expending Focus Points for the healing.

In addition, when you make an Unarmed Strike with Flurry of Blows and deal damage, you can use <span style="color:rgb(203, 123, 55)">Hand of Harm</span> with that strike without expending a Focus Point for Hand of Harm. You can still use Hand of Harm only once per turn.

You can use these benefits a total number of times equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum of once). You regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

#### <span style="color:rgb(203, 123, 55)">Level 17: Hand of Ultimate Mercy</span>  

Your mastery of life energy opens the door to the ultimate mercy. As a Magic action, you can touch the corpse of a creature that died within the past 24 hours and expend 5 Focus Points. The creature then returns to life with a number of Hit Points equal  
to 4d10 plus your Wisdom modifier. If the creature died with any of the following conditions, the creature revives with the conditions removed: [[Blinded]], [[Deafened]], [[Paralyzed]], [[Poisoned]], and [[Stunned]].

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest.
