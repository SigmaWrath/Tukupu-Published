> <span style="color:rgb(125, 125, 125)">*Protect nature and people from destruction.*</span> 

You stalk prey in the wilds and elsewhere, using your abilities as a Hunter [[Ranger]] to protect nature and people everywhere from forces that would destroy them.

### Mechanical Traits
#### <span style="color:rgb(203, 123, 55)">Level 3: Hunter's Lore</span>

You can call on the forces of nature to reveal certain strengths and weaknesses of your prey. While a creature is marked by your [[Ranger's Quarry]], you know whether that creature has any Immunities, Resistances, or Vulnerabilities, and if the creature has any, you know what they are.

#### <span style="color:rgb(203, 123, 55)">Level 3: Hunter's Prey</span>

You gain one of the following feature options of your choice. Whenever you finish a Short or Long Rest, you can replace the chosen option with the other one:

> **<span style="color:rgb(134, 93, 187)">Colossus Slayer</span>**: Your tenacity can wear down even the most resilient foes. When you hit a creature with a weapon, the weapon deals an extra 1d8 damage to the target if it’s missing any of its Hit Points. You can deal this extra damage only once per turn.
> 
> **<span style="color:rgb(134, 93, 187)">Horde Breaker</span>**: Once on each of your turns when you make an attack with a weapon, you can make another attack with the same weapon against a different creature that is within 5 feet of the original target, that is within the weapon’s range, and that you haven’t attacked this turn.

#### <span style="color:rgb(203, 123, 55)">Level 7: Defensive Tactics</span>

You gain one of the following feature options of your choice. Whenever you finish a Short or Long Rest, you can replace the chosen option with the other one:

> **<span style="color:rgb(134, 93, 187)">Escape the Horde</span>**: Opportunity Attacks have Disadvantage against you.
> 
> **<span style="color:rgb(134, 93, 187)">Multiattack Defense</span>**: When a creature hits you with an attack roll, that creature has Disadvantage on all other attack rolls against you this turn.

#### <span style="color:rgb(203, 123, 55)">Level 11: Superior Hunter's Prey</span>

Once per turn when you deal damage to a creature marked by your [[Ranger's Quarry]], you can also deal that ability’s extra damage to a different creature that you can see within 30 feet of the first creature.

#### <span style="color:rgb(203, 123, 55)">Level 15: Superior Hunter's Defense</span>

When you take damage, you can take a Reaction to give yourself Resistance to that damage and any other damage of the same type until the end of the current turn.
