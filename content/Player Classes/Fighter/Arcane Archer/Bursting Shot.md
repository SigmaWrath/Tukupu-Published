> *<span style="color:rgb(125, 125, 125)">You imbue the projectile with force energy drawn from the school of evocation.</span>* 

It detonates after it hits or misses the target. The target and all other creatures within 10 feet of it make a Dexterity save. A creature takes 3d6 Force damage on a failed save, half as much on a successful one. 

<span style="color:rgb(193, 145, 56)">The Force damage increases by 3d6 at level 10 (6d6) and at level 18 (9d6).</span>
