> *<span style="color:rgb(125, 125, 125)">You use abjuration magic to try to temporarily banish your target to a harmless location in the [[Feywild]]. </span>* 

The target must succeed on a Charisma save or be banished. While banished in this way, its Speed is 0, and it has the [[Incapacitated]] condition. At the end of its next turn, the target reappears in the space it vacated or in the nearest unoccupied space if that space is occupied.

<span style="color:rgb(193, 145, 56)">At level 10, the target also takes an extra 2d6 Force damage when the arrow hits it. This damage increases to 4d6 at level 18.</span>
