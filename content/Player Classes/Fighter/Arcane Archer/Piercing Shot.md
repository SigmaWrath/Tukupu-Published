> *<span style="color:rgb(125, 125, 125)">You use transmutation magic to give your projectile an ethereal quality.</span>* 

The target takes an extra 3d6 Piercing damage. After the projectile hits or misses the target it continues flying in a 30-foot Line, passing through objects and ignoring cover. Each creature in that Line must make a Dexterity save. A creature takes 3d6 Piercing damage on a failed save, half as much on a successful one. 

<span style="color:rgb(193, 145, 56)">The Piercing damage increases by 3d6 at level 10 (6d6) and at level 18 (9d6).</span>
