> *<span style="color:rgb(125, 125, 125)">Your enchantment magic causes this projectile to temporarily beguile its target.</span>* 

Choose one of your allies within 30 feet of the target. The target takes an extra 2d6 Psychic damage and makes a Wisdom save. On a failed save, it has the [[Charmed]] condition until the start of your next turn, with the chosen ally as the source. 

<span style="color:rgb(193, 145, 56)">The Psychic damage increases by 2d6 at level 10 (4d6) and at level 18 (6d6).</span>
