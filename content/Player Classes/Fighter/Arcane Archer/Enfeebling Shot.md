> *<span style="color:rgb(125, 125, 125)">You weave necromantic magic into your projectile.</span>* 

The target takes an extra 2d6 Necrotic damage and makes a Constitution save. On a failed save, all the target has the [[Poisoned]] condition until the end of its next turn. When a target is poisoned in this way, it subtracts 1d6 from the total damage of each attack it makes.

<span style="color:rgb(193, 145, 56)">The Necrotic damage increases by 2d6 at level 10 (4d6) and at level 18 (6d6). The target's damage reduction increases by 1d6 at level 10 (2d6) and at level 18 (3d6).</span>
