> *<span style="color:rgb(125, 125, 125)">When this projectile strikes its target, conjuration magic creates grasping, poisonous brambles, which wrap around the target.</span>* 

The target takes an extra 2d6 Poison damage and its Speed is reduced by 10 feet. The first time it moves on its turn without teleporting, it takes 2d6 Slashing damage. At the end of each of its turns, the target can make a Strength save. On a success, the effect ends. The brambles last for 1 minute, until you use it again, or if the target manages to escape early. 

<span style="color:rgb(193, 145, 56)">The Poison damage and Slashing damage both increase by 1d6 at level 10 (4d6) and at level 18 (6d6).</span>
