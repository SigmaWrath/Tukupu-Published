### Mechanical Traits

#### <span style="color:rgb(203, 123, 55)">Level 3: Open Mind</span>

As a Bonus Action, you can spend 1 Focus Point to activate one of the following effects:

> **<span style="color:rgb(134, 93, 187)">Sense Thoughts</span>**: You sense the presence of thoughts within 30 feet of yourself that belong to creatures that know languages or are telepathic. You don't read the thoughts, but you know that a thinking creature is present. 
> 
> This awareness lasts for 1 minute, or until your Concentration is broken.
> 
> **<span style="color:rgb(134, 93, 187)">Read Thoughts</span>**: Target one creature you can see within 30 feet of yourself or one creature within 30 feet of yourself that you detected with the Sense Thoughts option (if you choose the latter, you don't spend a Focus Point). You learn what is most on the target's mind right now. If the target doesn't know any languages and isn't telepathic, you learn nothing. 
> 
> If you use information you gained this way to make a Charisma (Deception, Intimidation, or Persuasion) check, you can instead roll the check as a Wisdom (Insight) check.
