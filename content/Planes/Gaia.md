> *<span style="color:rgb(125, 125, 125)">Gaia is the outer plane that embodies the forces of life and death.</span>*

Gaia is located at <span style="color:rgb(134, 93, 187)">Cardinal Planet</span>. It is accessible from its adjacent outer planes, the [[Beastlands]] and the [[Wildsea]] via [[Oceanus]] flowing through the [[Feywild]]. The [[Court of Stars]] also has [[Starcrossings]] into the Feywild. Gaia is also accessible via the [[Stargrave]] from the [[Astral Plane|Astral Sea]]. 



Gaia has three layers: the [[Feywild]], [[Vesperia]], and the [[Shadowfell]]. Each layer of Gaia has the same broad topography and natural landmarks. Scholars from Vesperia have long considered the Feywild and the Shadowfell as "echoes" of Vesperia.

###### <span style="color:rgb(203, 123, 55)">The Feywild</span>
*<span style="color:rgb(125, 125, 125)">(See main article: [[Feywild]])</span>*

The Feywild is the uppermost layer of Gaia, embodying vibrant chaotic energy and nature in its most whimsical state.
