> *<span style="color:rgb(125, 125, 125)">The set of [[Planes|planes]] in [[index|Tukupu]] that have continuous connection either directly or indirectly to the [[Vesperia|Material Plane]].</span>*

Ao is comprised of the following planes:
- [[Axial Planes]]
- [[Astral Plane]]
- [[Outer Planes]]

Ao was constructed and carved out from [[index|Tukupu]] by [[The Lovers]] in primordial times. Stable connections between the planes of Ao are only so because they are facilitated and maintained by [[The Lovers]]. 

Ao is kept separate from the other planes of the multiverse (aka, the [[Far Realms]]) by a [[Erwnvyn|boundary force]], although transient leaks do happen.
