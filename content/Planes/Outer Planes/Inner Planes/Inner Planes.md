> <span style="color:rgb(125, 125, 125)">*The inner planes are a semantic category of [[Planes|plane]] referring to [[Gaia]] and the [[Proximal Planes]].*</span>

The name [[Inner Planes]] is carried over from an outdated, [[Terracentric Cosmological Model|flawed cosmological model]] that placed [[Vesperia]] at the "center" of the cosmos.

The [[Proximal Planes]] and [[Gaia]] together form a unified construction that altogether behave like a single [[Outer Planes|outer plane]].

The inner planes are:
- [[Gaia]]
- [[Ethereal Plane]]
- [[Elemental Planes]]
