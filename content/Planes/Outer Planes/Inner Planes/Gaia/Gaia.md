> *<span style="color:rgb(125, 125, 125)">Gaia is the [[Outer Planes|outer plane]] that embodies the forces of life and death.</span>*

Gaia is located at <span style="color:rgb(134, 93, 187)">Cardinal Planet</span>. It is accessible from its adjacent outer planes, the [[Beastlands]] and the [[Wildsea]] via [[Oceanus]] flowing through the [[Feywild]]. The [[Court of Stars]] also has [[Starcrossings]] into the Feywild. Gaia is also accessible via the [[Stargrave]] from the [[Astral Plane|Astral Sea]]. 

Gaia has three layers: the [[Feywild]], [[Vesperia]], and the [[Shadowfell]]. Each layer of Gaia has the same broad topography and natural landmarks, which are synchronized. Scholars from Vesperia have long considered the Feywild and the Shadowfell as "echoes" of Vesperia.

Worth mentioning are [[Kami]], who have forms in each level of Gaia. 

###### <span style="color:rgb(203, 123, 55)">The Feywild</span>
<span style="font-style:italic; color:rgb(125, 125, 125)">(See main article: [[Feywild]])</span>

The Feywild is the uppermost layer of Gaia, embodying vibrant chaotic energy and nature in its most whimsical state. [[Solanah|The Sun]] and [[Akareth|the Moon]] chase each other across the sky, but the plane is always bathed in twilight as if the Sun were rising or setting. In general, the Feywild has a technicolor palette. 

###### <span style="color:rgb(203, 123, 55)">Vesperia - The Material Plane</span>
<span style="font-style:italic; color:rgb(125, 125, 125)">(See main article: [[Vesperia]])</span>

The Material Plane is the stable balance between the Feywild and the Shadowfell, and the middle layer of Gaia. Here, [[Solanah]] and [[Akareth]] chasing each other across the sky creates the familiar day/night cycle. 
###### <span style="color:rgb(203, 123, 55)">The Shadowfell</span>
*<span style="color:rgb(125, 125, 125)">(See main article: [[Shadowfell]])</span>*

The Shadowfell is the lowermost layer of Gaia, a bleak place of decay and death. There is a lack of color and light—no stars adorn the sky, and [[Solanah|the Sun]] and [[Akareth|the Moon]] are dark and would be unnoticeable if not for the very faint white rim pointing out their presence. Unprotected visitors would feel their life force draining away upon reaching this plane, and would turn to ash unless they left quickly.
