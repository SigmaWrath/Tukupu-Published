### Premise
Khorvayn the Conductor (he/him): an Artisan [[Dragons|Dragon]] who became a bard lich. Eight thousand years ago, before most recorded memory, Khorvayn imbued a Magnum Opus (Bard Dance) with a piece of his soul. As long as somebody somewhere remembered the tune, he couldn't die. And by performing this Magnum Opus as his swan song, he became immortal and undead—a lich. He could never perform his Magnum Opus again, instead relying on others to keep the memory (and him) alive. 

Khorvayn terrorized the world. Early humanity, halflings, dwarves, orcs, even elves—none were truly safe from him. Obsessed with growing his own power, he used his immortality to conquer, making the most horrific empire the realm had ever known. He killed off any who would dare threaten him, giants and humanoids and other dragons alike—until a small, tight-knit group of powerful friends (an adventuring party)—put a stop to it. Evading detection, they travelled the realm, seeking a way to take down the Conductor once and for all.

### Opening

> [!quote] Green storms are a perfectly mundane, non-magical phenomenon found in nature. When the sun is low on the horizon, the reddened sunlight scatters through the ice particles found in large thunderstorms, and what would normally come out the other end as a dull blue, is now a sharp green. And yet, the sun right now is high in the sky... so I [Professor Blup explains] am very curious as to how the storm above that island is green.
> 
> Grey seas reflect an overcast sky; our sights are set on the far horizon where we see a little swatch of greenish clouds looming over a tiny speck of an island. We pull back to find... the four of you, gathered around a large container of water (about *this* big), it has tiny little colorful plants dotting the bottom and floats a few feet above the wooden deck—most notably, occupying the container is a translucent orange, pink and white jellyfish. The *five* of you (four plus the jellyfish) look out over the port side railing at the prow of a massive frigate, which is moving at full speed with square sails fully unfurled. It's cold (you're in the northern seas after all) and the soundscape is messy—wind whips around your faces, the wooden planks of the ship creak as you cut through the sloshing ocean, and you can hear the distant roar of thunder. But that doesn't prevent you from hearing the jellyfish—Professor Blup—as they speak directly into your minds again:

> [!quote] Yes, strong magic often affects the environment around it—and of course *strong* magic would be needed to erase Qaqqaq Island from every map it's on. It's just... ah forgive my ramblings—we academics tend to do that, I'm sure your wizard is familiar. In any case, I expect we'll make landfall in about 3 hours—prepare anything you must before then—the crew will obviously assist you in anything aboard the ship, but you'll be exploring the island on your own. Oh, and the captain wanted to see you before you disembarked.
> And with that, Professor Blup floats away, leaving you to your own devices.

Have the players introduce any new characters. Describe how you're decked out for this adventure. Any preparations to do?

The atmosphere of the ship is excited, buzzing with anticipation. You have some time to chat and speculate before you reach the island. What do you talk about (in character)?
