### Act 1

It was *that* time of very early morning when our heroes waited for their ride. The sky was slowly fading from black to purple in anticipation of dawn. The air atop the small mountain plateau was cool and crisp, and the day ahead brimmed with the promise of possibility, excitement—adventure. As such, the waiting passengers were abuzz with conversation. [[Elves]], [[dwarves]], [[humans]], [[gnomes]], [[halflings]] even—the anticipation was palpable and the atmosphere lively. The concourse of the train station was carved entirely out of the stone of the mountain, with the train tracks running beyond the full width of the mountaintop plateau. They abruptly stopped several hundred feet out, hanging over the valley below. A marvel of arcane engineering, the Fey Line is an inter-dimensional "train" that takes passengers on a 36-hour journey from [[Vesperia]] (the Material Plane) to the [[Feywild]], to the realm of vibrant nature unrestrained, of chaos and emotion and magic. And it was on the passenger platform, waiting for this train, where our heroes first met.

<span style="color:rgb(134, 93, 187)">Mesmerelda</span> — a trigger-happy [[Jinxes|jinx]] [[ranger]]. <span style="color:rgb(134, 93, 187)">Alfvin</span> — a forest [[Gnomes|gnome]] [[Monk|monk]] who finds his zen by hitting things. <span style="color:rgb(134, 93, 187)">Kelser Cinderblock</span> — a rock [[Gnomes|gnome]] [[Fighter|fighter]] wielding a scimitar and a last name. <span style="color:rgb(134, 93, 187)">Jadhu</span> — a [[Fairies|fairy]] [[Warlock|warlock]] with mysterious patronage and absolutely zero filter. <span style="color:rgb(134, 93, 187)">Theivan</span> — a [[fuyohren]] [[Wizard|wizard]] from the land of [[Kaiju|kaijus]].

It was their tickets that brought them together. Small wooden cards, with words carved into them. They listed their names, species, and the date and time of the upcoming ride—specifically, they say `2568.0.8.3 PRC, Sunrise`. Their tickets also all said `Carriage: Spring; Camp: Forest Edge Riverbank, Waterfall`. 

As the first rays of the morning sun broke across the horizon, the Fey Line arrived with them. It materialized from mist onto the part of the tracks that hangs over the valley below the mountain (on the right side). When it stopped in front of you, you got a good look at it for the first time. The engine and four carriages all appear to be made out of some white crystal, and each of the four carriages are covered in vines and plants. The carriage just behind the engine features leaves of red, orange and yellow hues—it represents Autumn. The one behind it has icy, bare branches, and snow piled on top: the Winter Carriage. Behind that is Spring (your carriage): budding flowers, young green wood. The Summer Carriage bookends the train, covered with a meadow's worth of beautiful flowers in full bloom, and featuring a beehive.

Boarding the train was fairly quick as the staff's eyes glazed over their tickets, nodded them inside. The doors aren't *doors*, per se, so much as portals of mist that opened up on the side of each Carriage. And as our heroes stepped in, Mesmeralda noticed faint, glowing blue plant-tendrils mixed amongst the vines wrapping around the carriage.

When they stepped into the Spring Carriage, the sight was astounding. The sky is bright blue. A vast, technicolor forest spans most of the landscape, and to the right of it is a river, created by a roaring waterfall that flows down an 800-foot tall cliff. The river extends to the left of the forest as well, presumably flowing through it, and opens up into a lake. A couple of Giant Eagles soared overhead. Small camps, with firepits, wooden tables and chairs, etc., dot the landscape—a few around the lake, next to the river... On the far side of all of this, a wall of mist bounds the carriage, and when turning to look at the portal that passengers come through, indeed there is a plane of white mist. 

Staff members guided our heroes to a camp by the riverbank on the side of the forest with the waterfall, and brought them food as they requested (aside from Mesmerelda who had bottled cerebrospinal fluid). They talked and feasted, passing a couple of hour, sharing why they were headed to the Feywild.

Suddenly, they heard shouting in the distance. There were no other camps around, no other people... the shouting came from the top of the cliff. And then, they saw a *person*—knocked off, pushed off, flung off, *something* off the *edge* of the cliff. And they looked on in horror, as the person tumbled, down, down, down, the waterfall, landing in the spray that the waterfall throws up as it hits the surface of the river. And then the lifeless corpse, washed up onto the riverbank—about two hundred feet away from our heroes. They decided to investigate.

Upon investigating the scene at the bottom of the waterfall, our party found the corpse: an dark-skinned elf with intricately woven hair, wearing fine clothes. The blood on his elbow didn't come from him, but someone else. A tattoo on his neck signified he came from the [[Mikola Clan]], an agrarian community near the [[Waun Empire]]. Mikolans are known for being very strict about they sanctity of their minds, and are highly disciplined and place great emphasis on being rational. They also don't do drugs or ***drink alcohol***, among other things. 

After they finished investigating, Theivan looted a pouch of 42 gold pieces off the dead elf, before the party buried him with 2 gold pieces for passage in the [[Houses of Night]]. The party then proceeded up the cliff from where the dead elf fell, with Jadhu flying up using her fairy wings. Theivan cast [[Feather Fall]] to save the party from a climbing accident, and they all reached the top safely.

At the top of the cliff, they found a smashed bottle of blue glass. Kelser cast [[Mending]] in order to put it back together, and he was able to do so to read the label: Summer Honey. He also noticed a very faint blue thread on the mouth of the bottle—the same kind Mesmerelda noticed on the outside of the train.

The river at the top of the cliff led into a cave, and the party realized the only place for the perpetrator of the murder to have fled was into the cave. A small wooden boat bubbled up from the river, connected to the bank by a small rope. Using Theivan's ability to walk on water as a fuyohren and Jadhu's ability to fly as a fairy, the party sent their scouts ahead to determine the cave was safe. The remaining party members followed in the boat. As their boat drifted into the cavern mouth, the warmth of the Spring Carriage faded behind them. The river, once sparkling with golden sunlight and petals, darkened to a silvered reflection of the cave ceiling. And then: there was light—glowing, pale white crystals line the walls of the cave, and even diffuse light through the water from the riverbed below. The water looks like liquid moonlight, and the contrast of the light is how they noticed something drifting on the river's surface: a ticket, like the ones they used to board the train. The ticket said: `Name: Wu Ying; Species: Human; Date: 2568.0.8.3 PRC; Time: Sunrise; Carriage: Summer; Camp: Baobab Tree`. 

Further in the cave, there was a ledge of stone that protruded from the wall. On the ledge, was a large wooden chest, with a gold keyhole. The chest was unlocked, and the party suspiciously tried to open it, only for it to reveal another chest. They opened that one, and it revealed another chest. This continued, and after much annoyance of the party, a glittery weasel popped out of the chest, thanking them for their annoyance. The chests all collapsed back into the main wooden one with the golden keyhole, with a necklace inside. The necklace was made of clay beads, each representing a part of the train: the engine and the four carriages. The party talked to the weasel and learned that the necklace belonged to the weasel's owner, Lensa, a staff member, and that all staff members had necklaces like this. They can touch a bead and close their eyes to teleport to that cabin. They are able to teleport only to any place they've already visited, and they'll see a bird-eye view representation of the cabin (not real-time).

Theivan attuned to the necklace, by answering a question for themself: "what is the value in running away from a fight?". After answering "survival", she was able to attune. 

They rowed on, and the temperature plummeted. They could see their own icy breath when exhaling. Then, the cave opened up ahead to a snow white sky. Here was a vast landscape of mountainous glaciers, all bordering the icy river they rowed in which flowed through the center of all of it. Camps dotted the landscape, creatures sitting around firepits in the distance, and the wind carried faint echoes of laughter and conversation. They made it to the Winter Carriage.
