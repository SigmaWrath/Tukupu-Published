#### Premise
The Child has created shielded areas in [[Naltor|Maeve]], which have come to be known as the Havens. These Havens protect creatures inside from Nightmare attacks, and allow them to properly sleep. 

A solar [[Angels|angel]] foresees that further humanoid settlement into [[Naltor]] will disrupt the equilibrium of the place, and given that The Child's presence in Naltor is what allows these shields to be maintained, the angel wants The Child out of Naltor. She can't retrieve The Child herself because of [[Erwnvyn]]'s "nudge" rule. Thus, she gives this task to the party.

The Known Havens are as follows:
1. The Temple of [[Selashah]], where [[Cleric|clerics]] and acolytes worship the dream deity
2. Maeve Station, the train station for The Dream Line
3. Quartzkeep University Research Outpost 8, where academics head to research the secrets of the realm
4. Vonthrak's Magnificent Dream Resort, where two orcs run a resort for fabulously wealthy people
5. The Daydream (a tavern, only accessible with a *dream key*)

Since The Child is technically the "proper administrator" of the shield for each Haven, control of a Haven could technically be wrested away by force (e.g., by Nightmares). Thus, each Haven must have at least some defense against Nightmare attacks.
