> *<span style="color:rgb(125, 125, 125)">Tieflings are humanoids infused with the essence of the [[Fiendish Planes]].</span>*

### Overview

Tieflings are either born in the [[Fiendish Planes]] or have ancestors who originate there. Their connection to these planes is the tiefling's fiendish legacy, which comes with the promise of power but has no effect on the tiefling's moral outlook. 
##### <span style="color:rgb(203, 123, 55)">Infernal</span>
These tieflings contain the essence of the [[Infernal Planes]]. Horns, spines, tails, golden eyes, and a faint odor of sulfur or smoke are common physical features of tieflings, most of whom trace their ancestry to devils. 

#### <span style="color:rgb(203, 123, 55)">Abyssal</span> 
These tieflings originate from the entropy of the [[Abyssal Planes]]. Horns, fur, tusks, and peculiar scents are common physical features of such tieflings, most of whom have the blood of demons coursing through their veins.

#### <span style="color:rgb(203, 123, 55)">Chthonic</span> 
Some of these tieflings look cadaverous. Others possess the unearthly beauty of a [[succubus]], or they have physical features in common with a [[night hag]], or another fiendish ancestor from the [[Chthonic Planes]].

**<span style="color:rgb(134, 93, 187)">Darkvision</span>**: You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.

**<span style="color:rgb(134, 93, 187)">Fiendish Legacy</span>**: You are the recipient of a legacy that grants you supernatural abilities. Choose a legacy from the Fiendish Legacies table. You gain the level 1 benefit of the chosen legacy. You gain additional benefits as shown, when you reach character levels 3 and 5. 

You always have these spells prepared, and can cast them once without a spell slot per long rest. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spell-casting ability for the spells you cast with this.
