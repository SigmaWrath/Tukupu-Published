##### *<span style="color:rgb(203, 123, 55)">[[Rogue]] Class Feat (Level 1)</span>*

> *<span style="color:rgb(125, 125, 125)">You know how to strike subtly and exploit a foe’s distraction. Once per turn, you can deal an extra damage to one creature you hit with an attack roll if you have Advantage on the roll and the attack uses a Finesse or a Ranged weapon.</span>* 

The extra damage is listed in the Sneak Attack column of the Rogue Features table, and its type is the same as the weapon’s type.

You don’t need Advantage on the attack roll if at least one of your allies is within 5 feet of the target, the ally doesn’t have the [[Incapacitated]] condition, and you don’t have Disadvantage on the attack roll.
