##### *<span style="color:rgb(203, 123, 55)">[[Rogue]] Class Feat (Level 14)</span>*

You've practiced new ways to use your [[Sneak Attack]] deviously. The following effects are now among your [[Cunning Strike]] options:

> **<span style="color:rgb(134, 93, 187)">Daze</span> <span style="color:rgb(125, 125, 125)">(Cost: 2d6)</span>**: The target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw, or on its next turn, it can do only one of the following: move or take an action or a Bonus Action.
> 
> **<span style="color:rgb(134, 93, 187)">Knock Out</span> <span style="color:rgb(125, 125, 125)">(Cost: 6d6)</span>**: The target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw, or it has the [[Unconscious]] condition for 1 minute or until it takes any damage. The Unconscious target can repeat the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.
> 
> **<span style="color:rgb(134, 93, 187)">Obscure</span> <span style="color:rgb(125, 125, 125)">(Cost: 3d6)</span>**: The target must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw, or it has the [[Blinded]] condition until the end of its next turn.
