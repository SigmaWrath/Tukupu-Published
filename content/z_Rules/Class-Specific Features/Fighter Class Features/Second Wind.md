##### *<span style="color:rgb(203, 123, 55)">[[Fighter]] Class Feat (Level 1)</span>*

> *<span style="color:rgb(125, 125, 125)">You have a limited well of physical and mental stamina that you can draw on. As a Bonus Action, you can use it to regain Hit Points equal to 1d10 plus your Fighter level.</span>*

You can use this feature a number of times as shown in the Fighter Features table. You regain one expended use when you finish a Short Rest, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
