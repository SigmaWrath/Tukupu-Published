##### *<span style="color:rgb(203, 123, 55)">[[Fighter]] Class Feat (Level 1)</span>*

> *<span style="color:rgb(125, 125, 125)">You have honed your martial prowess and gain a [[Fighting Style]] feat of your choice.</span>*
