##### *<span style="color:rgb(203, 123, 55)">[[Fighter]] Class Feat (Level 9)</span>*

> *<span style="color:rgb(125, 125, 125)">If you fail a saving throw, you can reroll it with a bonus equal to your Fighter level. </span>*

You must use the new roll, and you can’t use this feature again until you finish a Long Rest.
