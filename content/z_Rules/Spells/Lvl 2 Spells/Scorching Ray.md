##### *<span style="color:rgb(203, 123, 55)">Level 2 - Evocation</span>*

|Casting Time|Range|Components|Duration|
|---|---|---|---|
|Action|120 feet|V, S|Instantaneous|

You hurl three fiery rays. You can hurl them at one target within range or at several. Make a ranged spell attack for each ray. On a hit, the target takes 2d6 Fire damage. 

***<span style="color:rgb(134, 93, 187)">Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot</span>***: You create one additional ray for each spell slot level above 2.
