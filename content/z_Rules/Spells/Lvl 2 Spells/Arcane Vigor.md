##### *<span style="color:rgb(203, 123, 55)">Level 2 - Abjuration</span>*

|**Casting Time**|**Range**|**Components**|**Duration**|
|---|---|---|---|
|Bonus Action|Self|V, S|Instantaneous|

You tap into your life force to heal yourself. Roll one or two of your unexpended Hit Point Dice, and regain a number of Hit Points equal to the roll's total plus your spellcasting ability modifier. Those dice are then expended.   

<span style="color:rgb(134, 93, 187)">**_Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot_**</span>: The number of unexpended Hit Dice you can roll increases by one for each spell slot level above 2.
