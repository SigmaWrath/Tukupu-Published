##### *<span style="color:rgb(203, 123, 55)">Level 2 - Enchantment</span>*

|Casting Time|Range|Components|Duration|
|---|---|---|---|
|Action|60 feet|V, S, M (a straight piece of iron)|[[Concentration]], up to 1 minute|

Choose a Humanoid that you can see within range. The target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or have the [[Paralyzed]] condition for the duration. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the spell on itself on a success. 

***<span style="color:rgb(134, 93, 187)">Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot</span>***: You can target one additional Humanoid for each spell slot level above 2.
