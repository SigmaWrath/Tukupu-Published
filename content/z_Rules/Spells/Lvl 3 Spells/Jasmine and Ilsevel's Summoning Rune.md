##### *<span style="color:rgb(203, 123, 55)">Level 3 - Conjuration</span>*

| Casting Time         | Range                     | Components                                         | Duration   |
| -------------------- | ------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| Action or [[Ritual]] | Touch, a willing creature | S, M (spell ink prepared with [[Nightcap]] spores) | Indefinite |

> This spell was invented by two wizards in love named Jasmine Iyeri <span style="color:rgb(125, 125, 125)">(she/her)</span> and Ilsevel Valfaren <span style="color:rgb(125, 125, 125)">(she/her)</span> in the year `2565 PRC`. It can only be learned through one of them, by a [[Wizard|wizard]].

You draw an intricate rune on a willing creature. The rune is unique to you. When the creature presses the rune and speaks your name, you and up to 5 allies you choose can appear next to the creature if you consciously choose to do so. After the rune is activated, the ink disappears from the creature. 

<span style="color:rgb(192, 82, 75)">This spell only works once per lifetime per pair of caster/recipient (which are differentiable roles), unless its first cast is in the [[Houses of Night]].</span>
