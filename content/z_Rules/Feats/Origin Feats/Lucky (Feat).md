You gain the following benefits:

> **<span style="color:rgb(134, 93, 187)">Luck Points</span>**: You have a number of Luck Points equal to your Proficiency Bonus and can spend the points on the benefits below. You regain your expended Luck Points when you finish a Long Rest.  
> 
> **<span style="color:rgb(134, 93, 187)">Advantage</span>**: When you roll a d20 for a D20 Test, you can spend 1 Luck Point to give yourself Advantage on the roll.  
> 
> **<span style="color:rgb(134, 93, 187)">Disadvantage</span>**: When a creature rolls a d20 for an attack roll against you, you can spend 1 Luck Point to impose Disadvantage on that roll.
